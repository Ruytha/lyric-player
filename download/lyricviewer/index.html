Synced lyrics for the songs you play, the way Apple Music shows them: your own music, or whatever’s playing in Spotify and Apple Music on your PC.

Free · Windows 10 and 11

What it does

Play a song in Spotify or Apple Music and its cover and synced lyrics show up in Lyric Player, in time.

It finds the lyrics for you

It searches AMLL TTML DB, BiniLyrics, NetEase, QQ Music and LRCLIB, plus Apple Music when you sign in, and picks the version that matches your song: same title and artist, same length, word-synced when it exists.

Not the right one? Press / and pick another.

The cover moves. The lyrics stay.

When an album has a moving cover on Apple Music, Lyric Player plays it. In a wide window the tall version can fill one side while the lyrics run on the other, like Apple Music on iPad.

Apple Music Mode

One key turns the window into a portrait player like the iPhone’s lyrics view. Hide the lyrics for the Now Playing screen, where an album’s tall animated cover fills the top.

Mac This computer

The Mac version isn’t ready to download yet. Until then, open Lyric Player in your browser.

Apple siliconM1 and later · IntelOlder Macs ·
First time opening it on a Mac The app isn’t from the App Store or notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks before opening it:
  1. Open the .dmg and drag Lyric Player to Applications.
  2. Open it. When macOS says it can’t check it, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. If it says the app “is damaged”, run this once in Terminal, then open it again:
    xattr -cr "/Applications/Lyric Player.app"
  4. To follow Spotify or Apple Music, allow it when macOS asks if Lyric Player may control them.
