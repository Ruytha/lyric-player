Mac

Your computer

macOS 12 or later · real Liquid Glass on macOS 26

Apple siliconM1, M2, M3, M4 and later · IntelOlder Macs ·
First time opening it on a Mac The app isn’t from the App Store or notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks before opening it:
  1. Open the .dmg and drag Lyric Player to Applications.
  2. Open it. When macOS says it can’t check it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. If it says the app “is damaged”, run this once in Terminal, then open it again:
    xattr -cr "/Applications/Lyric Player.app"
  4. To follow Spotify or Apple Music, allow it when macOS asks if Lyric Player may control them.

What it does

Everything the Apple Music lyrics screen does, for any song.

🎤

Word-by-word lyrics

Words light up as they’re sung, with background vocals, duets and the springy motion of Apple Music.

🎧

Follows Spotify and Apple Music

Play a song in another app and its cover and synced lyrics show up here, in time.

🔎

Finds the lyrics for you

Searches several lyrics libraries and picks the best match, word-synced whenever possible.

🌈

A background that moves

The iPhone’s flowing cover background, ported from Lyricify, plus animated album covers.

🪟

Floating lyrics

The line being sung, floating over your other windows and games. Clicks go straight through.

🌏

Translations and romanization

See what Japanese, Korean or Chinese songs mean, and how to sing along.
