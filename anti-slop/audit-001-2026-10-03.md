Date: 2026-10-03
Subject: `private-videos/Lyric Player - showcase (private).mp4` (45 s, 60 fps), built from `show.html`
Mode: after (audit first, fix only the numbers the owner approves)
Skills applied: antislop (core), antislop-ui, antislop-copywriting, design-taste-frontend, high-end-visual-design, redesign-existing-projects

Direction chosen by the owner for the remake: **bold and kinetic**. Near-black base, the song's cover colour as a single wash, huge headline words that cut on the beat, every scene composed differently. Dials ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 3.

Priority follows the antislop rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

## Checked and passing

- R-17 / R-38: no invented numbers. "45 seconds", "2.10" and the BPM-matched AutoMix are real; every feature named exists in the app.
- R-18: no testimonials.
- R-23: the app icon and Ruytha's avatar were supplied by the owner.
- R-25: measured on the rendered frames at the brightest background each text crosses: song label 4.67:1, scene counter 3.5:1 (24 px bold, large text, needs 3:1), side paragraphs 7.01:1, captions on a 70% dark chip. All pass.
- Identity motif: the word-by-word sweep in the intro headline is the app's own lyric effect. Keep it.
