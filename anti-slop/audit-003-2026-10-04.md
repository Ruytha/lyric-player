# Audit 003: Lyric Player app UI

Date: 2026-10-04
Subject: the player (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `apple-ui.css`, UI strings in `src/*.js`), desktop and web
Mode: after (audit first, fix only the numbers the owner approves)
Skills applied: antislop (core), antislop-ui, antislop-copywriting, apple-design (Apple HIG: accessibility.md, typography.md, color.md, liquid-glass.md, settings.md, writing guidance)

Direction: the app's identity is the owner's product goal, an Apple Music style lyrics player (cover-tinted background, word-by-word lyrics, Liquid Glass controls). R-30 does not apply: looking like Apple Music is the brief. The audit keeps that look and checks it against the rules and against Apple's own guidelines.

Priority follows the antislop rule tier (Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW), raised where Apple's accessibility guidance treats it as critical.

## Findings

| # | Priority | Rule | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HIGH | R-25, `accessibility.md › Color and effects` ("Up to 17 pts: 4.5:1") | Secondary text is 30-45% white on a background that takes the cover's colour. Measured: 45% white is 4.14:1 on a dark panel (#2a2a30) but 2.44:1 on the olive a yellow cover gives (#6e6a2c); 40% is 2.24:1, 32% (the "Lyrics from" line after the last lyric) 1.93:1. Affects about 15 styles: setting hints, song lengths in Find lyrics and the queue, library counts, source names, stats notes, What's new dates, credits notes, equalizer labels, field placeholders. |
| 2 | HIGH | R-32, `accessibility.md › Mobility` (keyboard alone) | Focus rings are removed without a replacement on the settings sliders (`.set-slider:focus-visible { outline: none }`), the window buttons, and three text fields (search boxes, settings text inputs). A keyboard user can't see where focus is on those. |
| 3 | HIGH | `typography.md` (macOS minimum 10 pt), `accessibility.md › Text size` | 9 px text: the "1" on the repeat-one button. 10 px: the drop hint on the cover in Apple Music Mode. Both under the desktop minimum. |
| 4 | HIGH | R-02 | 12 messages people see use an em dash: "No lyrics saved for this song — drop a .ttml file…" (2 places), "No lyrics found for this song — press / …" (2), "Couldn't look for lyrics (offline?) — press / …" (2), "Lyrics found on … — press / to pick different ones", "These lyrics have no timing — showing static lyrics.", "Welcome back — …", the window title "Song — Artist", the mini player's separator, and the phone remote's page title. |
| 5 | MEDIUM | `liquid-glass.md › Accessibility states`, `accessibility.md` | No answer to Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast (`prefers-reduced-transparency`, `prefers-contrast` are never used). With glass on, people who turned transparency off in the system still get blurred, refracting controls. |
| 6 | MEDIUM | R-10, `liquid-glass.md › Restraint` ("limit it to the most important functional elements") | Liquid Glass (blur, 1.9x saturation, refraction) is applied to 9 kinds of control at once: round buttons, icon buttons, menus, sheets, toggles, stepper buttons, segmented controls, sheet buttons and search panels. Glass inside glass happens in every sheet (segmented control and buttons on a glass sheet). |
| 7 | MEDIUM | `settings.md › Desktop (macOS)` ("the standard Command–Comma (,) keyboard command") | Settings open with S or from the ••• menu, but not with Ctrl+, / Cmd+,, the shortcut every desktop app uses for settings. |
| 8 | LOW | `writing.md` capitalization, antislop-copywriting consistency | Menu items mix styles: sentence case ("Find other lyrics…", "Load new song…", "Use still cover") next to names in title case ("Apple Music Mode", "Send to AMLL TTML DB…"). Apple uses title-style capitalization for menu items and buttons. |

## Checked and passing

- R-17 / R-18 / R-36 / R-38: no invented numbers or claims; every control does what it says.
- R-24 / R-26: every menu item and button has a real action (checked against `onMenuAction`).
- R-27: lyrics have loading ("Looking for lyrics…"), empty ("No lyrics saved…") and error ("Couldn't look for lyrics…") states; library and queue have empty states.
- Control sizes: icon buttons are at least 30 px, round buttons at least 34 px, above the 28 pt desktop default (`accessibility.md › Mobility`).
- Reduce Motion: honoured (`prefers-reduced-motion` in both stylesheets, plus Still background in Settings).
- Unlit lyric lines at 32% white are a deliberate state, as in Apple Music: the line being sung is the content, the others are context. Not counted as a contrast failure; Increase Contrast (finding 5) is the place to brighten them.
- Liquid Glass can already be turned off in Settings › Interface.
