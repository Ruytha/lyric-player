# Audit 004: player revamp, closer to Apple Music

Date: 2026-10-04
Subject: the player (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `apple-ui.css`), standard layout, desktop and web
Mode: after (audit first, fix only the numbers the owner approves)
Owner's brief: "Closer to real Apple Music"; what bothers them most: "Looks dated / plain"
Skills applied: antislop (core), antislop-ui, apple-design (`menus.md`, `lists-and-tables.md`, `liquid-glass.md`, `playing-audio.md`, `settings.md`)
Evidence: fresh offscreen captures at 1280x800 over アイドル (yellow cover): player, ••• menu, Settings, Library.

What already matches Apple Music and stays: the cover-coloured moving background (Lyricify port), the cover that shrinks on pause and springs back on play, word-by-word lyrics with blurred other lines, the scrubber that thickens while dragged, the scrolling title for long names, round glass star and ••• buttons, the filled transport glyphs.

So the dated feel does not come from the Now Playing screen itself. It comes from everything that opens on top of it: menus, sheets and lists look like stacks of separate cards, and the menu has outgrown the screen.

## Findings

| # | Priority | Rule | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HIGH | `menus.md › Submenus` ("Use submenus sparingly"), `menus.md` small/medium layout | The ••• menu holds 22 items in one list. At 1280x800 it runs off its own platter and scrolls (it cuts off at Equalizer; Sleep Timer, Stats, Load New Song and everything after are hidden). Apple Music's ••• menu fits on screen. The top row has 4 labelled tiles; Apple's medium layout is 3 labelled items (4 is the small layout, icons only). Fix: 3 tiles (Card, Clip, Full Screen), 4 short groups, and the rarely used tools in two submenus ("Lyrics Tools ›": Edit Lyric Timing, Tap to Sync, Send to AMLL, Lyric Offset; "Open ›": Load New Song, Load Lyrics File, Find Album Cover, Use Still Cover). Every item keeps working; nothing is removed. |
| 2 | HIGH | `liquid-glass.md › Restraint` (no glass on glass), `lists-and-tables.md › Best practices` | Menu rows, library rows and settings groups each sit on their own filled rounded tile inside a glass panel. The result is a stack of cards inside a card, which is what reads as dated next to iOS 26 / macOS 26. Apple: one platter, plain rows, hairline separators that start at the text (not under the artwork or icon), highlight only on hover or press. |
| 3 | MEDIUM | R-35 (finished craft) | Dark smears inside the glass panels: one behind the INDUSTRY BABY and Cake rows in the library, one behind Lyric Offset in the menu. Both sit over the cover and its shadow, so the panel's blur or refraction is picking them up. Fix: find the layer and stop it showing through. |
| 4 | MEDIUM | `playing-audio.md`, Apple Music's own Now Playing | The row under the volume has 5 buttons (Lyrics, Settings, AirPlay/Cast, Library, Full Screen). Apple Music has 3: Lyrics, AirPlay, Up Next. Settings is already in ••• and on Ctrl+,; Full Screen is in the ••• top row. Fix: Lyrics, AirPlay/Cast, Library, spaced like Apple's. |
| 5 | MEDIUM | `settings.md`, `lists-and-tables.md` (grouped style) | Settings rows are tall: 6 settings fill the whole sheet, so finding one means long scrolling. Each slider row repeats its hint under it. Apple's grouped style: one line per row (label left, value or switch right), sliders on their own row with small min/max symbols, and the explanations moved to the group footer. Same settings, roughly half the height. |
| 6 | MEDIUM | Apple Music library lists | Library rows: title in semibold, the playing song marked with a pink "♪" text character, row hover fills the whole tile. Apple Music: title regular weight, artist dimmed, cover with small rounded corners, and the playing song shows the animated bars symbol in the accent colour (still when paused). |
| 7 | LOW | `menus.md` › Icons ("uniform visual treatment") | Menu icons are thin line drawings of mixed size and stroke (clock, hand, microphone with a slash), next to filled transport glyphs on the player. Apple uses one symbol style with filled and outline variants at one weight. Fix: redraw the menu and bottom-row icons at one stroke weight and optical size. |
| 8 | LOW | Apple Music Now Playing | Opening the menu, Settings or Library is a fade and scale from one corner. Apple Music grows menus out of the ••• button and slides sheets up with a spring, and the player behind dims slightly. Fix: menu grows from its button; sheets and library slide in; a light dim behind. Reduce Motion keeps a plain fade. |

## Not changed

- The Now Playing layout, lyrics, background, colours and fonts (they already match Apple Music; changing them would move away from the brief).
- Apple Music Mode (the tall phone layout): unchanged, apart from shared fixes 2, 3 and 7.
- No feature is removed. Items leaving the bottom row and the menu's top level stay reachable in the menu, by shortcut, or in a submenu.
